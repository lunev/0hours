# Testing instructions (Chrome Web Store reviewer field, max 500 chars)

No login required. Click the icon, tap Activate, then Settings to configure Voice Language (flag grid), Volume (drag slider, tap Play to instantly preview the chime without waiting an hour), and Silence Mode (toggle to reveal a quiet-hours range). All changes save instantly. Permissions: storage (settings), alarms (hourly chime schedule), offscreen (MV3 workers can't play audio directly). No servers or accounts involved.
