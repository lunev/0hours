No login required. Click the icon, tap Activate, then Settings: Voice Language (flag grid), Volume (slider, tap Play to preview instantly), Silence Mode (quiet-hours range), and Muted Pages (add a site; chime skips while that page is open). Permissions: storage (settings), alarms (chime schedule), offscreen (MV3 can't play audio directly), tabs (checks active tab URL for Muted Pages). No servers/accounts.
